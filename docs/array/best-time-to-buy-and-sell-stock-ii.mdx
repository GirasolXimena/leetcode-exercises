# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock II

## The problem

Given daily prices, make as much profit as possible while holding at most one share. You may complete multiple transactions, but a transaction must buy before it sells.

## Approach

Every positive increase from one day to the next can be collected. Adding `tomorrowPrice - price` whenever tomorrow is higher is equivalent to holding through a rising run, and it naturally skips decreases. The algorithm is greedy because every profitable adjacent increase is available without preventing a later transaction.

The scan takes $O(n)$ time and $O(1)$ extra space. Tests cover rising, falling, flat, empty, and one-day prices.
